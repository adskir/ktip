Cookie Settings

Last updated: October 7th, 2026

This page explains which cookies may be used on ktiphairextension.com and how you can control them.

1. Strictly necessary

Needed for the website to work (for example, loading pages securely over HTTPS). These cannot be switched off.

2. Analytics and advertising

Set through Google Tag Manager to measure visits and the performance of ads (Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta). They help us understand how the site is used.

3. Embedded content

The Google Maps map and links to Instagram may set their own cookies under their providers' policies.

4. How to manage cookies

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. You can also opt out of personalized Google ads at adssettings.google.com. To opt out of the sale or sharing of your information, use Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information.
